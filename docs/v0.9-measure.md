14-day/three-clean-drill beta, independent hardware or an RPO/RTO commitment.

- Windows `10.0.26200.9974` x64, NTFS
- pinned restic 0.19.1
- 64 MiB plus 100 small files (101 files, 67,109,254 bytes)
- five runs per build, one host, OS page cache not controlled;

## Limitations

- No CPU, peak RSS, transfer-byte or staging-byte instrumentation.
- No controlled cold-cache or storage-emulator runs.
- One host, one filesystem and synthetic content; no 14-day cadence, outage,
  reconnect or low-space scenario.
- Restore outliers (12.9–14.0 s) are retained, not discarded.
- The measurement corpus is much smaller than the 5 GiB/50k prerequisite.

## Gate

V0.9 measurement harness: present, with debug and release observations. V0.9
beta: **not started**. The 14-day observation window, three clean-device
restores, injected failures, second operator and independent hardware remain
open.
